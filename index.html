The Art of Making

From Hide to Heirloom

Every pair begins as a conversation between artisan and material. Over 200 hours of deliberate hands transform raw Italian leather into footwear that carries the quiet weight of mastery.

01

Selection

Each hide is hand-selected from heritage tanneries in Tuscany. We choose only full-grain leather — the outermost layer that bears the natural markings of the animal's life. These imperfections are not flaws; they are proof of authenticity.

02
02

Pattern & Cut

Our master cutter studies the grain direction, thickness, and character of each hide before making the first incision. Every pattern piece is positioned to maximize both beauty and structural integrity — a process that cannot be rushed, emphasized by Dubee's exceptional years of expertise in the shoemaking industry.

03

Stitching

Blake stitched by hand using waxed linen thread. Each stitch is individually tensioned — tight enough for permanence, loose enough for the leather to breathe. The result is a seam that flexes with the foot, not against it.

04

Lasting

The shoe is pulled over a hand-carved last — the wooden form that defines its silhouette. Our lasts are shaped over decades, refined with each generation of makers. This is where the shoe finds its soul.

05

Finishing

Hand-burnished with natural waxes and oils, each pair develops a patina unique to its owner. The leather darkens and softens with wear, telling a story that only time and care can write.
